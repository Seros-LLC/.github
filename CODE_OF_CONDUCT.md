# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Seros projects are working spaces. Everyone taking part — maintainers, contributors,
customers, contractors — is expected to be straightforward, considerate, and honest.

**Expected:** clear, direct communication; criticism of ideas and code rather than people;
accepting a decision once it is made; asking when you do not know; crediting others' work.

**Not accepted:** harassment or discrimination of any kind; sexualised language or imagery;
personal or political attacks; publishing anyone's private information; sustained disruption
of discussions; encouraging any of the above.

## Scope

Applies in all Seros repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and in any public
space where someone is representing Seros, LLC.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to **conduct@seros.dev**. Reports are handled by the maintainers of
Seros, LLC, kept as confidential as practical, and answered within five business days.

Consequences escalate with severity and repetition: a private correction, a public warning,
a temporary ban from interaction, then a permanent ban. Serious conduct — threats,
harassment, doxxing — can start at the top of that list.

If a report concerns a maintainer, and you are not comfortable using the address above,
send it to hello@seros.dev marked "conduct — confidential".
